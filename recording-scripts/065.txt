A country mouse invited his city cousin to visit, and served him a plain meal of beans and bacon and a crust of bread.
The town mouse laughed at such humble fare and begged his cousin to come and see how well he lived.
In the city they dined on the leftovers of a grand feast, but again and again they were forced to run and hide as people and dogs burst into the room.
Goodbye, said the country mouse at last. I would rather eat my simple meal in peace than feast in fear.
A crust eaten in safety is better than a banquet enjoyed in danger.
