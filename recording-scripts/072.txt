In the evenings I usually head to the library on the second floor, where it stays quiet well past dark.
I claim a desk near the window, spread out my notes, and put my phone in my bag where I can't reach it.
I work in stretches of about forty-five minutes, then get up, walk a lap, and refill my water before starting again.
It's amazing how much I get through when there's nothing around to pull my attention away.
