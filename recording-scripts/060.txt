A dog, crossing a bridge over a stream with a piece of meat in his mouth, saw his own reflection in the water.
Thinking it was another dog with another piece of meat, he made up his mind to have that also.
So he snapped at the shadow in the water, but as he opened his mouth the piece of meat fell out, dropped into the stream, and was never seen again.
Beware lest you lose the substance by grasping at the shadow.
