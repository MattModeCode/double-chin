There's a small park a few streets over that I visit whenever I need to think.
A gravel path loops around a shallow pond, and in the summer the whole edge is lined with tall reeds.
Ducks paddle around in the shade, and there's an old wooden bench under a willow tree that almost no one uses.
I'll sit there for half an hour with a notebook, watching the water, and by the time I leave my head feels a great deal clearer.
