A lion was once asleep in the forest when a little mouse ran across his face and woke him.
The lion was angry and caught the mouse in his great paw, ready to eat him.
Pardon me, cried the little mouse. Forgive me this time, and I shall never forget it.
Who knows but I may be able to do you a good turn one of these days?
The lion was so amused that he lifted his paw and let him go.
Some time later the lion was caught in a hunter's net.
The little mouse heard his roar, came, and gnawed through the ropes until the great beast was free.
