A very tall oak was uprooted by the wind and thrown across a stream.
It fell among some reeds, and it said to them, I wonder how you, who are so light and weak, are not entirely crushed by these strong winds.
The reeds replied, You fight and contend with the wind, and so you are destroyed; while we, on the other hand, bend before the least breath of air, and therefore remain unbroken, and escape.
Stoop to conquer, and bend so that you do not break.
