A shepherd boy watched a flock of sheep near a village, and thought it would be great fun to trick the villagers.
So he shouted, Wolf! Wolf! and when the people came running he laughed at them.
He did this more than once, and each time the villagers found no wolf at all.
But one day a wolf really did come.
The boy cried out in terror, Wolf! Wolf! but not a soul came to help him, and the wolf made a fine meal of the flock.
A liar will not be believed, even when he speaks the truth.
