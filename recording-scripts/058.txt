A crow, half dead with thirst, came upon a pitcher which had once been full of water.
But when the crow put its beak into the mouth of the pitcher, it found that only a little water was left in it, and that it could not reach far enough down to get at it.
It tried and tried, but at last had to give up in despair.
Then a thought came to the crow. It took a pebble and dropped it into the pitcher.
Then it took another pebble and dropped it in. Little by little the water rose, until at last the crow was able to drink.
Necessity is the mother of invention, and little by little does the trick.
