One hot summer's day a fox was strolling through an orchard when he came to a bunch of grapes just ripening on a vine which had been trained over a high branch.
Just the thing to quench my thirst, said he.
Drawing back a few paces, he took a run and a jump, and just missed the bunch.
Turning round again with a one, two, three, he jumped up, but with no greater success.
Again and again he tried after the tempting morsel, but at last had to give it up, and walked away with his nose in the air, saying that he was sure the grapes were sour.
It is easy to despise what you cannot get.
