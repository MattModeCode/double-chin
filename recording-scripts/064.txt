A man and his wife had the good fortune to own a goose that laid a golden egg every single day.
Lucky though they were, they soon began to think they were not getting rich fast enough.
Imagining the bird must be made of gold inside, they decided to kill it and take the whole treasure at once.
But when they cut it open, they found it was just like any other goose.
So they had neither a store of gold nor any more golden eggs.
Those who have plenty want too much, and lose the little they already hold.
