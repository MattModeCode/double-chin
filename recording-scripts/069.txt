On the weekend I try to step away from screens for a while.
I'll cook something that takes a bit of effort, usually a big pot of soup or a tray of roasted vegetables that lasts a few days.
In the afternoon I might read, or fix something that's been on my list, or just take a long walk if the weather holds.
It isn't exciting, but there's a quiet satisfaction in a slow, ordinary day that goes exactly to plan.
