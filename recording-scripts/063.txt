A milkmaid was going to market carrying her pail of milk balanced on her head.
As she walked along she began daydreaming.
The money from this milk will let me buy three hundred eggs. The eggs will hatch into chickens, and I shall sell the chickens and buy a fine new dress.
When I go to the fair, all the young men will admire me, but I shall toss my head and refuse them every one.
At that thought she tossed her head, and down came the pail, and all the milk spilled onto the ground.
Do not count your chickens before they are hatched.
