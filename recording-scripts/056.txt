The ants were spending a fine winter's day drying grain collected in the summer time.
A grasshopper, perishing with famine, passed by and earnestly begged for a little food.
The ants asked him why he had not treasured up food during the summer.
He replied that he had not the leisure, for he had passed the days in singing.
They then said in derision that if he was foolish enough to sing all the summer, he must dance supperless to bed in the winter.
It is best to prepare for the days of necessity.
